Source and scope

Loan application event log from a Dutch financial institution, released as BPI Challenge 2017 by Boudewijn van Dongen. Applications were filed in 2016; events continue into early 2017.

Known boundaries

Historical data from one institution. No employee cost, lending profitability, funded-loan confirmation or intervention outcomes are established. Rework associations are descriptive.

Raw source data remains unchanged. Zero requested amounts are treated as unknown in analytical tables. Timestamp ties use original trace order. Multiple offers are aggregated before application-level joins.
